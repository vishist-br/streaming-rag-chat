# Tidewater Robotics Employee Handbook

This handbook summarises the policies that apply to all full-time employees.

## Leave

### Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year and must be used by 31 March. Leave requests longer than 5 consecutive days need manager approval at least three weeks in advance.

### Sick leave

Employees may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

### Parental leave

Tidewater offers 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave to every new parent, regardless of gender. It can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Wednesdays are a shared office day for all teams. Working from another country is allowed for up to 30 days per year and needs approval from the people team because of tax rules.

## Expenses

### Travel

Book flights in economy class for journeys shorter than 6 hours. The hotel budget is 180 euros per night. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 euros per day.

### Claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase. A receipt is required for any item over 25 euros. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary run.

### Home office

New employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 500 euros. Laptops are replaced every 3 years.

## Learning

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 euros for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Employees may also spend 4 hours per week on self-directed learning.

## Security

All laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Report a lost or stolen device to the security team within 1 hour. Passwords must be stored in the company password manager and never shared in chat.
